A two-line explanation of a queue for a 10-year-old, built on one concrete analogy.

## The entry

> A queue is like pouring marbles into a funnel: plenty rattle in at the wide top, but only one can drop out of the narrow neck at a time, in the order they went in.
> It maps the one-at-a-time wait and the first-in-first-out order, but it leaves out that people can chat, step out, or get grumpy while waiting, which marbles never do.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node) writes exactly those two lines to `/output/entry.txt` — deterministic, no network, no randomness, no timestamps. Line 1 explains a queue in a single vivid sentence through one analogy a 10-year-old can see and hold: marbles rattling into a funnel. Line 2 is the honest accounting: it names what the analogy maps (one person served at a time, in arrival order) and what it leaves out (people can chat, step out, or get grumpy — marbles can't).
